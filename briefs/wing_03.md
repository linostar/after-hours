# Wing 3 brief: A Sunday on La Grande Jatte (Seurat)

## Constraints
- Three rooms; 6 guards (the ramp: 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 12 across the wings); 3 to 6 minutes; every star reachable (bot-proved).
- Every room past the first teaches nothing new in words: each needs one trick, proved by the bot's ablation.
- Gimmick: Seurat's dotted floors keep the mime's footprints (`PRINT_LIFE` 6 s, wiped by a catch); a
  guard who sees them follows the trail. Parasols cast shade: in it a guard cannot see the mime, even in his cone.

## Depth pass (feat/polish-depth)
- Room 0, the lawn (easel, exit low left): trick **shade**. A guard sweeps the crossing to the right
  door; there is no wait in which the crossing is clear, so the mime stands in the parasol's shade while
  the cone passes over it, then goes on.
- Room 1, the hall: trick **trail lure**. A sentry paces before the far door. The mime walks a loop onto
  the dotted floor away from the door; the sentry follows the prints off his post and the mime cuts
  through behind him.
- Room 2, the gallery (masterpiece): trick **lure**. A sentry stands the swap row; tilting the frame at
  the far end of the back wall, out of his cone, pulls him off while the mime swaps.
- Sketch: inside the gallery's portrait (Mona Lisa). Stepping in drops the sketch on the floor below.
